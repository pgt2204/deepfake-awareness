What Are Deepfakes?

Deepfakes are images, videos or audio that have been created or changed using Artificial Intelligence. They can make someone appear to say or do something they never actually said or did.

🎬 Watch and Understand Deepfakes

Discover how AI-generated videos can look realistic and why verifying content is important.

Remember: Always verify the source before trusting or sharing digital content.

🎭 Face Manipulation

AI can change a person's face in a video or image to make it look like someone else.

AI can generate audio that sounds like a real person's voice.

AI can modify video expressions, movements and speech.

Stay Safe in the Digital World

Follow these safety tips to protect yourself from misleading and manipulated digital content.

🔍 Verify Information

Always check the source and confirm information before believing it.

🚫 Don't Share Blindly

Avoid forwarding suspicious images, videos or audio without verification.

🔒 Protect Personal Data

Avoid sharing personal photographs, videos and sensitive information with unknown sources.

📰 Use Trusted Sources

Cross-check news and online content with reliable sources.

⚠️ Stay Alert

Be careful with unexpected messages, suspicious links and unusual requests.

📢 Report Suspicious Content

Report misleading or harmful content through the appropriate platform.

Report and Get Help

Found suspicious or manipulated content? Follow these steps to respond responsibly.

📸 Save Evidence

Keep the original link and relevant details of the suspicious content.

🚫 Don't Share

Avoid forwarding or spreading unverified content.

📢 Report Content

Use the reporting option available on the social media platform.

👮 Get Official Help

In India, you can report cybercrime through the official government portal.
