Stay Safe in the Digital World

Follow these tips to protect yourself from deepfakes and online misinformation.

🛡️ Verify Information

Always check the authenticity of information before trusting it.

🚫 Don't Share Blindly

Avoid forwarding suspicious photos, videos or audio messages.

🔐 Protect Personal Data

Keep your personal information and private media secure.

🌐 Use Trusted Sources

Prefer official websites and reliable news sources.

👀 Stay Alert

Think critically about unusual or shocking online content.

📢 Report Suspicious Content

Use the platform's reporting tools when you encounter misleading content.
