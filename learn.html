Learn About Deepfakes

Deepfakes are AI-generated or manipulated images, videos and audio that can make people appear to say or do things they never actually did.

Types of Deepfakes

Face Manipulation

AI changes or replaces a person's face in an image or video.

Voice Cloning

AI creates an artificial voice that sounds like a real person.

Video Manipulation

AI modifies video content to create misleading scenes.
